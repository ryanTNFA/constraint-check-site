    Provenance

    How we test it. The plan has three parts: a retrospective against published constraints analyses using only the data available at the time, a pre-registered head-to-head against diagnostics now in progress, and a panel of economists given the same information. The first part's development run was done on 2 November 2026: on 35 analyses the tool wrongly ruled out 6 of 100 binding constraints and said “cannot tell” for 87 percent of areas. Its sealed holdout has not been run, and the other two parts have not started. Treat every rating as a starting point.
